Animated buttons

Loading and feedback states for the Harness button, plain CSS. Click any button to run its state; most reset on their own.

1 · Spinner in place

The label goes transparent and a 14px ring spins in the middle. Width never changes, so nothing around it shifts. This was the shipped aria-busy look; it now survives only on icon-only buttons.

Trigger
aria-busy="true"
Motion
700ms linear, loop

2 · Spinner slides in

For actions that take a while, the label stays legible and a ring grows in beside it. The button widens with an eased grid-column transition, not a width jump. Chosen as the default aria-busy look, shipped in design-system/css/blocks/button.css via ::before so no markup changes.

Trigger
data-busy-style="inline"
Motion
240ms ease-out-expo, then loop

3 · Label swap, then confirm

Three labels stacked in one grid cell: idle, busy and done. The width is the widest of them, so the swap is a vertical slide with no reflow. Done turns Confirm Green for a moment and then goes quiet again.

Trigger
data-state="busy|done"
Motion
160ms fade, 240ms slide, 1.2s hold on done

4 · Determinate progress

When the work reports progress, the fill grows from the left edge and a mono percentage counts up. The fill is an 18% ink layer over the variant colour, so it works on teal, white and red alike.

Trigger
style="--progress: 42"
Motion
background-size, 240ms ease-out per step

5 · Edge sweep

Indeterminate but quieter than a spinner: a 2px bar sweeps along the bottom edge while the label stays as it is. Good for toolbar actions where a spinner would be loud.

Trigger
data-busy-style="edge"
Motion
1.1s ease-out, loop

6 · Border trace

A short teal highlight runs around the border. The one place a secondary button is allowed a trace of signal, since it means working, same as a running session dot.

Trigger
data-busy-style="trace"
Motion
1.2s linear, conic-gradient via @property

7 · Hold to confirm

For irreversible actions a press-and-hold replaces the confirm dialog. The fill grows linearly for 1.2s while the pointer is down; letting go early retracts it in 160ms. Completing it fires the action and flashes Confirm Green.

Trigger
data-hold, :active
Motion
1200ms linear in, 160ms out

Press and hold with the mouse, or hold Space with the button focused.

8 · Micro-interactions

The resting states, for reference: hover lays a 5–8% ink veil in 90ms, active nudges 1px down, arrows lead by 2px on hover. Disabled drops to 45% and animates nothing.

Trigger
:hover, :active, :disabled
Motion
90–160ms ease-out
